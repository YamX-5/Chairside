## Rules

1. **Log the asset here in the same change that adds the file.** A licence
   discovered later is a licence discovered too late.
2. **CC0 is preferred** — no attribution, no obligations, no risk at launch.
3. **CC BY is acceptable** but the attribution must land in the game's credits
   screen, not only in this file.
4. **Never ship** anything whose licence forbids commercial use, or which was
   generated by a service whose free tier makes outputs public. Chairside is
   going to be paid (50 JOD lifetime / 5 JOD monthly), so non-commercial and
   share-alike licences are both disqualifying.
5. Adobe Stock requires a Stock subscription, which this account does not have —
   licensing returns "Get started with a free trial". Do not plan around it.
